Delete Your Data

Last updated: 1 August 2026

Ephix Pulse does not store your personal data on any server. Your watchlist, filters, preferences and cached content are stored entirely on your device. There is no account, no login and no cloud sync. Deleting the data on your device deletes it completely.

Android App

Option 1 — Clear app data (keeps the app installed)

  1. Open Settings on your device.
  2. Tap Apps (or Apps & notifications).
  3. Find and tap Ephix Pulse.
  4. Tap Storage & cache.
  5. Tap Clear storage (or Clear data), then confirm.

This removes your watchlist, cached images and all locally saved preferences. The app will behave as if freshly installed.

Option 2 — Uninstall the app

  1. Long-press the Ephix Pulse icon on your home screen or app drawer.
  2. Tap Uninstall and confirm.

Uninstalling removes all app data from your device automatically.

Website (ephix.net)

The website stores a small amount of data in your browser's local storage (e.g. your saved list and sound preference). To remove it:

  1. Open your browser's developer tools (F12 or Ctrl + Shift + I).
  2. Go to the Application tab.
  3. Under Storage, click Clear site data.

Alternatively, clearing your browser's cookies and site data for ephix.net in your browser settings will achieve the same result.

Push Notification Tokens

If you enabled push notifications, a push token (a delivery address for your app install) is stored on our server. This is not tied to your identity. To remove it:

  • Turn off notifications in the app, or
  • Uninstall the app — tokens that stop working are automatically discarded.

Google Analytics (Website Only)

The website uses Google Analytics. This data is anonymous and aggregated. It is not linked to any account or identity because the site has none. You can prevent collection by using a browser ad-blocker or enabling "Do Not Track".

Contact

If you believe any data relating to you is held and would like it removed, contact us at info@ephix.net and it will be handled promptly.
